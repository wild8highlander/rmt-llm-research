# 📜 monograph — Derived-Theorems Edition

<p align="center">
  <em>12 chapters · 2 languages · every theorem verified numerically</em><br><br>
  <a href="../assets/logos/formal_proof.banner.svg"><img src="../assets/logos/formal_proof.banner.svg" width="640" alt="formal proof banner"></a>
</p>

A single coherent text that derives the derivable part of the
first-principles program — and is explicit about the boundary. Both
editions carry identical mathematics: an assumption register, numbered
theorems with proofs or exact citations, and the verification numbers
produced by the experiment suite.

| Edition | File | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| 🇬🇧 English | [`monograph_EN.md`](monograph_EN.md) | 1–12 |
| 🇷🇺 Русский | [`monograph_RU.md`](monograph_RU.md) | 1–12 |

Per-chapter sources (identical content, chapter-per-file):

* [`chapters_EN/`](chapters_EN/) — 01 scope · 02 layer operator · 03 Silverstein/MP · 04 free probability · 05 BBP · 06 outliers & overlaps · 07 hallucination split · 08 entropy response · 09 Tracy-Widom · 10 optimal filtering · 11 verification · 12 references
* [`chapters_RU/`](chapters_RU/) — те же главы по-русски

**Reading order.** Chapter 1 (scope + assumptions) is mandatory; the
derivation chain is 2 → 3 → 5 → 6 → 10 with 4, 8, 9 as satellites; 11
collects every number.
